{% extends "base.html" %} {% load distribution specimens ui %} {% comment %} Entry grid: allocate specimens to one line of an approved request. Candidates are the available specimens of the line's type (and study) from queries.candidates, one page at a time; a blocked candidate's checkbox is disabled with its recorded reasons beside it. Each checkbox carries the specimen's revision as read for this page (value "id:revision"); the service rechecks it, every blocker and the line's count under lock.
