What is built.
What follows.

OpenBGA Biorepository is developed in the open. This roadmap lists what is built and what follows it.

It carries no dates. Milestones 0 to 5 are built, from storage to the distribution of specimens to other institutions; the order after that can change when repositories say otherwise.

Built

The journey
works.

Milestones 0 to 5 make the complete path work end to end: from a storage unit to a specimen that was disposed of or sent to another institution, with every step in custody history and the audit trail. Equipment and quality records cover excursions, inventory audits, deviations and staff training. Use synthetic data only while the project is in development.

Milestones 0 to 5: what is built
MilestoneWhat it brings
0 · PlatformSign-in with optional two-factor authentication, announcements on the sign-in page, four staff roles checked on the server, the shell and theme in light and dark, an attributed append-only audit trail, help for every page, tests, a container image and a deployment description. Back to the roadmap
1 · StorageSites, rooms, units, racks, shelves, boxes and positions; container types with grids and numbering schemes; templates; occupancy views, free-position search, blocked positions and box or rack moves with their contents. Back to the roadmap
2 · SpecimensRegistration with an opaque identifier, aliquots and derivatives, placement, check-out and check-in with computed time out of storage, observed thaws, holds, and disposal with a second person's approval and a filtered request register CSV. Back to the roadmap
3 · ThroughputLabels with barcodes and scan-to-lookup, CSV import with a validation pass, filters and exports, a full synthetic demo and a browser journey test. Back to the roadmap
4 · Governance and distributionStudies that govern collections, consent records by scope with withdrawal and escalation to destruction while retaining holds, requests decided by a second person, allocation checked against the records, pick lists, shipments with the shipper's declarations and manifests, receipt confirmation, returns held for review, agreements, and a distributed status with time away kept apart from time out of storage. It records what the shipper declares and what the repository's records say; it never classifies a shipment or decides consent, and nothing is emailed. Back to the roadmap
5 · Equipment and qualityEquipment records, service events and due dates, LN2 level and fill logs, temperature readings filterable by recorded limits, including readings with neither bound, and reviewed excursions with explicitly selected manual holds; box inventory audits with manual or normalized scanner CSV observations and a filtered register export; reviewed deviations with a filtered register CSV; and staff training assignments with completion and assessment evidence, explicit due dates, retained corrections and a filtered register CSV. The repository defines its limits, training requirements and renewal schedule. Back to the roadmap
