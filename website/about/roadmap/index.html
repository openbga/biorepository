OpenBGA Biorepository is developed in the open. This roadmap lists what is built and what follows it.

It carries no dates. Milestones 0 to 5 are built, from storage to the distribution of specimens to other institutions; the order after that can change when repositories say otherwise.

Now

The journey
works.

Milestones 0 to 5 make the complete path work end to end: from a storage unit to a specimen that was disposed of or sent to another institution, with every step in custody history and the audit trail. Equipment and quality records cover excursions, inventory audits, deviations and staff training. Use synthetic data only while the project is in development.

Milestones 0 to 5: what is built
MilestoneWhat it brings
0 · PlatformSign-in with optional two-factor authentication, announcements on the sign-in page, four staff roles checked on the server, the shell and theme in light and dark, an attributed append-only audit trail, help for every page, tests, a container image and a deployment description.
1 · StorageSites, rooms, units, racks, shelves, boxes and positions; container types with grids and numbering schemes; templates; occupancy views, free-position search, blocked positions and box or rack moves with their contents.
2 · SpecimensRegistration with an opaque identifier, aliquots and derivatives, placement, check-out and check-in with computed time out of storage, observed thaws, holds, and disposal with a second person's approval and a filtered request register CSV.
3 · ThroughputLabels with barcodes and scan-to-lookup, CSV import with a validation pass, filters and exports, a full synthetic demo and a browser journey test.
4 · Governance and distributionStudies that govern collections, consent records by scope with withdrawal and escalation to destruction while retaining holds, requests decided by a second person, allocation checked against the records, pick lists, shipments with the shipper's declarations and manifests, receipt confirmation, returns held for review, agreements, and a distributed status with time away kept apart from time out of storage. It records what the shipper declares and what the repository's records say; it never classifies a shipment or decides consent, and nothing is emailed.
5 · Equipment and qualityEquipment records, service events and due dates, LN2 level and fill logs, temperature readings filterable by recorded limits, including readings with neither bound, and reviewed excursions with explicitly selected manual holds; box inventory audits with manual or normalized scanner CSV observations and a filtered register export; reviewed deviations with a filtered register CSV; and staff training assignments with completion and assessment evidence, explicit due dates, retained corrections and a filtered register CSV. The repository defines its limits, training requirements and renewal schedule.

Next

Set the
next priority.

Milestones 0 to 5 are built. No further milestone is committed: the later and exploring items below need a repository that wants them and can test them. Physical scanner qualification, training requirements and real-world validation remain the repository's responsibility.

Later

Beyond
the milestones.

Not started, and each needs a repository that wants it. Requests are entered and decided by staff today, and nothing is emailed or polled.

  • Monitoring and alarms

    • Taking in readings from storage monitoring and raising alarms

  • Requester accounts and wider access

    • An investigator portal for requests, billing and permissions scoped to several sites

Exploring

Open
questions.

Directions the project is weighing. Each needs a repository that wants it and can test it.

Standards exports
Exports in published specimen description formats such as SPREC and MIABIS.
Electronic signatures
Signed approvals, deferred until the audit trail and roles are proven in use.
Spatial storage maps
Future research into visual layouts of buildings, floors and rooms, with cabinets, freezers and tanks placed in their physical context. Drill down to racks, boxes and positions to locate a specimen, while preserving inventory and custody controls. Research optional building and floor records versus labels or a flexible hierarchy, with a migration that retains existing identifiers and history. Not implemented or assigned a delivery priority or date.

Help set
the order.

Tell the project what your biorepository needs. Feedback from repositories moves items on this page.

OpenBGA Biorepository supports a repository's own records and quality system. It does not certify compliance or accreditation, and nothing on this page is a commitment to a date.
