Now

The journey
works.

Milestones 0 to 4 make the complete path work end to end: from a storage unit to a specimen that was disposed of or sent to another institution, with every step in custody history and the audit trail. Use synthetic data only while the project is in development.

Milestones 0 to 4: what is built
MilestoneWhat it brings
0 · PlatformSign-in with optional two-factor authentication, announcements on the sign-in page, four staff roles checked on the server, the shell and theme in light and dark, an attributed append-only audit trail, help for every page, tests, a container image and a deployment description.
1 · StorageSites, rooms, units, racks, shelves, boxes and positions; container types with grids and numbering schemes; templates; occupancy views, free-position search, blocked positions and box or rack moves with their contents.
2 · SpecimensRegistration with an opaque identifier, aliquots and derivatives, placement, check-out and check-in with computed time out of storage, observed thaws, holds, and disposal with a second person's approval.
3 · ThroughputLabels with barcodes and scan-to-lookup, CSV import with a validation pass, filters and exports, a full synthetic demo and a browser journey test.
4 · Governance and distributionStudies that govern collections, consent records by scope with withdrawal, requests decided by a second person, allocation checked against the records, pick lists, shipments with the shipper's declarations and manifests, receipt confirmation, returns held for review, agreements, and a distributed status with time away kept apart from time out of storage. It records what the shipper declares and what the repository's records say; it never classifies a shipment or decides consent, and nothing is emailed.

Next

Equipment
and quality.

Milestone 5 has started: equipment records, excursions and box inventory audits with manual entry or scanner CSV import are built. The rest follows, in this order unless repositories ask otherwise.

  • Equipment and quality

    • Built: equipment records, service events and due dates, the liquid nitrogen level and fill log, temperature readings and reviewed excursions; box inventory audits with manual entry or normalized scanner CSV import, reviewed observations and saved discrepancies
    • Scanner import accepts the project CSV format for one box at a time; device-specific exports need conversion and physical scanner qualification remains with the repository.
    • To come: deviations and training, and holds placed from an excursion

Later

Beyond
the milestones.

Not started, and each needs a repository that wants it. Requests are entered and decided by staff today, and nothing is emailed or polled.

  • Monitoring and alarms

    • Taking in readings from storage monitoring and raising alarms

  • Requester accounts and wider access

    • An investigator portal for requests, billing and permissions scoped to several sites

Exploring

Open
questions.

Directions the project is weighing. Each needs a repository that wants it and can test it.

Standards exports
Exports in published specimen description formats such as SPREC and MIABIS.
Electronic signatures
Signed approvals, deferred until the audit trail and roles are proven in use.
Withdrawal escalation
Turning a withdrawal that stopped use into one that asks for destruction, without a second record; today a manager reinstates the consent and records the new withdrawal.

Help set
the order.

Tell the project what your biorepository needs. Feedback from repositories moves items on this page.

Vapor Phase supports a repository's own records and quality system. It does not certify compliance or accreditation, and nothing on this page is a commitment to a date.
