From a temperature excursion, staff can select the affected specimens and place disposal, use or all-use holds with a reason, up to 200 at a time. Managers release these holds from the specimen record; closing or reopening the excursion leaves them in place.

## Technical notes

No migration. The selection is rechecked when submitted and expires after one hour. No temperature triggers a hold automatically.
